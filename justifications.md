Redis: Deployment is the right call here, on purpose. I didn't give it a PersistentVolumeClaim at all — no volumes: block, no disk. That matches the real app's design: Redis is just a transient queue, worker drains it continuously, and the original docker-compose doesn't persist it either. StatefulSet's entire value proposition (stable per-replica identity + a durable volume that follows that identity across rescheduling) doesn't apply to a pod with no state to preserve. If you want Redis to survive a pod restart without losing whatever votes are still sitting unprocessed in the queue, that's a real gap worth closing — and at that point, yes, it should become a StatefulSet too. Say the word and I'll add that.
